Privacy Policy & Terms

The REVA University Student Portal operates as the internal grievance resolution platform. Information collected including Student Roll Numbers (SRNs), academic blocks, and photo attachments are strictly utilized for facilities operations and departmental maintenance.

No personal information is transferred to third parties. All ticket logs remain stored in the secured REVA University database for audit and compliance.

Terms and Conditions

1. Students must provide factual and genuine details regarding campus facilities or services.

2. Falsified claims or abusive language in submissions will lead to administrative review under university guidelines.

3. Service level resolution windows vary based on replacement parts availability and departmental emergency workloads.
